THE PROBLEM & THE SOLUTION

From Manual Lead Qualification to Automated Sales Follow-Up

Website inquiries often require manual qualification, repeated questions, and follow-up before a sales team can identify promising opportunities.

This project connects an AI chatbot with n8n to capture lead information, summarize conversations, and notify the sales team when meaningful funnel events occur.

⚡

Manual Lead Qualification

Sales teams spend time reviewing inquiries, gathering business details, and identifying which prospects are ready for a conversation.

✦

AI Chatbot + n8n Automation

The chatbot gathers business context, while n8n routes events, retrieves stored lead information, and sends structured notifications through Telegram.

FROM CONVERSATION TO SALES CONTEXT

How the AI Chatbot & n8n Automation Works

A connected workflow that turns chatbot conversations into structured lead information and real-time sales notifications.

  1. ✦01

    AI Chatbot Qualification

    Context-aware questions capture the visitor’s business, challenges, tools, goals, and qualification signals.

  2. ✦02

    Supabase Data Storage

    Lead profiles, session information, and conversation context are stored for later retrieval.

  3. ⚡03

    n8n Event Routing

    A webhook receives the event. A Switch node selects a branch and retrieves lead, profile, and session data.

  4. ✦04

    Telegram Sales Notifications

    Structured alerts give the sales team conversation context and visibility into booking-related intent.

Three Events. One Automated Workflow.

The webhook and Switch node route each event independently to its matching notification branch.

↗ / 01

Conversation Summary Ready

conversation_summary_ready

Retrieves lead context and sends a Telegram notification when a conversation summary is ready.

↗ / 02

Booking Offered

booking_offered

Routes the discovery-call recommendation event to a booking-offer notification.

↗ / 03

Booking Clicked

booking_clicked

Sends a booking-intent notification when the visitor clicks the booking CTA.

A booking click signals intent; it does not confirm a completed appointment.
