AUTOMATION JOURNEY

Step-by-Step Automation Journey

From the first visitor interaction to final booking-intent notifications and sales follow-up.

01
ENTRY POINT

Visitor Opens the Website

The visitor lands on the redesigned TechQuarters AI website and opens the floating AI chatbot through a Start Chat button or chatbot launcher.

02
QUALIFICATION

AI Qualification Conversation

The AI asks intelligent questions about business/company, industry, biggest problem, current workflow, CRM/tools, traffic source, timeline, goals, and optional budget.

03
LLM OUTPUT

AI Response Generation

The LLM generates a natural language reply, lead profile, buying signals, conversation summary, recommended next action, and qualification stage.

04
DATABASE

Data Saved to Supabase

The system stores the lead, chat session, messages, lead profile, AI summary, buying intent, scoring signals, and funnel events.

05
EVENT CREATED

Conversation Summary Trigger

Once enough details are collected, the AI creates a complete business summary and triggers conversation_summary_ready.

06
N8N AUTOMATION

n8n Automation

n8n receives the event through a webhook, retrieves the lead, profile, and session details, then sends a formatted Telegram summary to the sales team.

07
CONVERSION SIGNAL

Booking Recommendation

When the AI determines the lead is highly qualified, it recommends a discovery call and creates booking_offered. The CTA appears in the chat experience.

08
FINAL ALERT

Booking Click Tracking

When the visitor clicks “Book a Strategy Call,” the system creates booking_clicked. n8n sends the final Telegram alert and opens Calendly.

09
CALENDLY BOOKING

Discovery Call Booking Flow

The high-intent visitor is redirected to Calendly, selects a preferred schedule, and moves from qualification to a confirmed business conversation.

N8N EVENT ROUTING

Event-Driven n8n Automation

The workflow listens for high-value funnel events, retrieves the latest lead context from Supabase, and sends real-time Telegram notifications for faster sales follow-up.

Automation Active

One centralized workflow handles conversation summaries, booking offers, and booking-click events—giving the sales team complete lead context exactly when action is needed.
